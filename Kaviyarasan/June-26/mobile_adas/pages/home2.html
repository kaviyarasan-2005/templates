The Danger of Misalignment

Even a 1-degree shift in a front camera or radar angle can result in the vehicle scanning yards off-center at highway speeds.

Why Precise Pitch, Yaw, and Roll Matter

ADAS sensors rely on geometric reference points. After a minor fender-bender, wheel alignment, or windshield replacement, the baseline coordinate system changes. Our tools recalibrate these systems to the millimeter.

  • Static Calibration: Controlled shop floor alignment using mechanical lasers.
  • Dynamic Calibration: Active road learning sessions via vehicle OBD connection.
