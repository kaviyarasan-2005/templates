The Botanist's Log

Project Details

Client

Dr. Amelia Vance

Date

March 2026

Materials

Italian Olive-tanned Calfskin, 300gsm Arches Cold-Pressed Watercolor Paper, Waxed Linen Thread, Brass Hardware.

Binding Style

Modified Long-Stitch with Wrap

Request Similar Build

The Challenge

Dr. Vance, a field botanist researching alpine flora in the Cascades, required a journal that could withstand harsh weather, accommodate heavy watercolor washes without warping, and expand to hold pressed plant specimens without breaking the spine.

The Solution

We opted for a thick, weather-resistant calfskin sourced from Italy, left un-dyed so it would naturally darken and scar from fieldwork, creating a unique patina over time.

The binding is a modified long-stitch. We engineered the spine to be slightly wider than the text block, allowing the book to swell comfortably as specimens are added. The paper is 100% cotton rag, heavy enough to handle wet-on-wet watercolor painting in the field without buckling.

The Embellishment

Finally, we hand-tooled a custom fern motif onto the front cover using heated brass styluses, a nod to her primary area of research.
