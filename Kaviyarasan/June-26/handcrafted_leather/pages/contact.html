Because every journal is bound by hand to order, our standard turnaround time is 2-3 weeks from the date of purchase. For custom commissions requiring bespoke brass dies, please allow 4-6 weeks.

Due to the highly personalized nature of our custom and monogrammed journals, we cannot accept returns on these items. However, if there is a defect in the craftsmanship, please contact us within 7 days of receipt and we will repair or replace it.
