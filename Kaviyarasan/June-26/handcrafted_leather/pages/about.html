Our Heritage

Founded in 1982, BIND was born from a singular obsession: preserving the dying art of traditional bookbinding in an increasingly disposable world.

Our Philosophy

Slow Craft

We reject mass production. Every journal is made to order, taking hours of meticulous, uninterrupted focus.

Lifelong Durability

A journal should outlast its author. We build our books to become heirlooms, surviving centuries on a shelf.

Sustainable Practice

We source only vegetable-tanned leathers and acid-free, FSC-certified papers with zero plastic packaging.

Hand-Bound vs. Machine

Commercial books are held together by cheap glues that crack and pages that eventually fall out. Our bindings rely on the physical integrity of waxed linen thread and the tension of a master's hands.

We sew directly through the fold of the signatures (the sections of paper). This guarantees the book will lay completely flat when opened—a necessity for writers and artists alike.

Material Ethics

We believe true luxury shouldn't cost the earth. Our leather is a byproduct of the food industry, tanned using natural bark extracts rather than harmful chromium chemicals. Our papers are acid-free to prevent yellowing, produced in mills powered by renewable energy.
