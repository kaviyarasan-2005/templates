Archival Paper Selection

We select papers that resist bleed-through, feathering, and yellowing over time.

Sketch (120gsm)

A slight tooth, perfect for graphite, charcoal, and light ink.

Ruled & Dotted (100gsm)

Smooth finish for fountain pens, available in 7mm rule or 5mm dot grid.

Mixed Media (160gsm)

Heavyweight paper handling light washes, markers, and gouache.

Watercolor (300gsm)

Cold-pressed, 100% cotton rag paper for heavy wet media.

Cover Embellishments

Blind Embossing

Deeply pressed initials or logos without foil, creating a subtle, tactile shadow in the leather.

Foil Stamping

Gold, silver, copper, or rose gold foil pressed into the leather using heated brass type.

Custom Brass Dies

We can convert your artwork or family crest into a custom brass die for stamping.

Binding Systems

Exposed Coptic

Braided stitching exposed on the spine. Lays 100% flat.

Long-Stitch Wrap

Sewn directly through a slotted cover with a leather tie wrap.

Refillable Ring / Spiral System

A leather binder housing standard 6-ring inserts or spiral notebooks for endless reuse.
